Our Story

CareFIRST Surgery was established with a simple but powerful mission: to provide accessible, high-quality healthcare to the people of Witbank/Emalahleni and the wider Mpumalanga region. Under the leadership of Dr. V. Prefa, our practice has grown into a trusted healthcare provider, serving individuals and families across the community.

With over 17 years of clinical experience spanning multiple countries, Dr. Prefa brings a wealth of medical knowledge and a deep commitment to patient care. Our team of dedicated healthcare professionals shares a passion for compassionate medicine, ensuring every patient receives the attention and treatment they deserve.

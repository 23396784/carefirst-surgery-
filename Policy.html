Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information when you use our services:

  • Identity information: Full name, date of birth, ID/passport number, gender
  • Contact information: Phone number, email address, physical address
  • Medical information: Medical history, current conditions, test results, prescriptions, treatment records
  • Medical aid information: Scheme name, membership number, plan details
  • Financial information: Billing details for payment processing

How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is processed for the following purposes:

  • Providing medical consultations, diagnosis, and treatment
  • Managing appointments and patient records
  • Processing medical aid claims and billing
  • Communicating with you about your healthcare
  • Issuing medical certificates, referrals, and prescriptions
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Improving the quality of our healthcare services

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the following lawful grounds under POPIA:

  • Consent: You have given your consent for the processing
  • Contractual obligation: Processing is necessary to provide you with medical services
  • Legal obligation: We are required to keep medical records as prescribed by law
  • Legitimate interest: Processing is necessary for the proper management of our practice

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Your information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With other healthcare providers for referral or continuity of care
  • With your medical aid scheme for claims processing
  • With laboratories or diagnostic service providers as part of your treatment
  • When required by law, regulation, or a court order
  • With your explicit consent

Data Retention

We retain patient medical records in accordance with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) guidelines and the National Health Act. Medical records are retained for the required statutory periods. After this period, records are securely destroyed.

Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information we hold
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include physical security of our premises, restricted access to patient records, and secure storage of electronic data.
